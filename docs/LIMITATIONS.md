# Limitations

The results concern a deterministic two-object collision simulator, the packaged model family, the tested velocity and position edits, and the reported seeds. Rank 4 is the smallest passing rank under this protocol; it is not asserted to be universal. The event-phase result is a bounded negative development result, not evidence that phase information never exists. Position-edit failures do not imply that every position intervention must fail.

The joint-velocity composition analysis is development-only. It supports bounded joint capacity and exact affine coefficient composition on the tested domain, but it does not establish a universal velocity coordinate system, fresh-checkpoint transfer, or full target-specific joint control. Its specificity boundary does not weaken the primary single-component confirmatory result and does not show that the rank-4 carrier lacks joint information.
